Aegis

Aegis is a web browser for Windows that doesn’t collect your data: it blocks ads and trackers, and it doesn’t connect to anything on its own until you decide. It protects a lot, but it doesn’t hide everything: websites still see your IP address, it isn’t Tor Browser, and no blocker is completely invisible.
